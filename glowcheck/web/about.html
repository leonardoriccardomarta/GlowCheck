GlowCheck

GlowCheck is a web app for cosmetics. You photograph an ingredient list (INCI). We score that list against the skin type and goal you report, then show a simple result and a cheaper alternative when the match is weak.

GlowCheck is not a doctor, not a lab, and not medical advice. It does not scan your face.

The first scan is free. Lifetime access is €9.99 once, paid with Stripe. You can sign in with email or Google to keep your shelf on another phone.

Come funziona
