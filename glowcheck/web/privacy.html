Privacy policy

Last updated: 15 September 2026

GlowCheck compares a cosmetic INCI list with the skin type and goal you report. It is not medical advice and we do not analyse faces.

What we store

  • On your device: skin quiz, saved scans, and whether you have lifetime access.
  • On our servers, if you create an account: name, email, sign-in provider (email or Google), password hash if you use email, and lifetime-access status.
  • Label photos are sent to our analyze server only to read the ingredient list. We do not use them to identify you.

Google Sign-In

If you tap Continue with Google, Google shares your name and email with us so we can create or open your account. We do not get your Google password.

Payments

Lifetime access costs €9.99 once. Payment is handled by Stripe. We do not store your card number. Stripe may receive your email for the receipt.

We do not sell your data

To delete on-device data, clear this site’s data or reset from your profile. To delete an account, email aiorchestrator@gmail.com.
